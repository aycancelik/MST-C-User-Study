Which heatmap is easier to read?

AI models can show which parts of a photo they focused on, as a coloured "heatmap". For each photo you'll see two heatmaps side by side. Just click the one you find more legible, meaning clearer and easier to read.

  • ⏱ about 5 minutes
  • 🖼 42 short comparisons
  • 👆 one click each

Step 1 of 4 · What you'll see

A photo and two heatmaps

On every page you'll see a photo at the top. Below it are two heatmaps of that same photo, made by an AI model. Each one highlights the parts of the photo the model paid attention to.

Every page looks like this. Only the photo and the heatmaps change.

Step 2 of 4 · Reading a heatmap

Colours show where the model looked

againstignoredfor

Each heatmap covers the same area as the photo. Red and blue mark regions the model reacted to; grey means it ignored them.

The heatmaps can look very different from each other, some smooth, some patchy, some speckled or almost empty. That's expected.

Step 3 of 4 · Your task

Click the heatmap you find more legible

For each photo, look at the two heatmaps and click the one that is more legible to you, meaning the one that's clearer and easier to read.

The question won't be repeated on every page. Just keep doing the same thing: one click, and the next pair appears straight away.

  • There are no right or wrong answers. We want your impression.
  • Don't overthink it. A few seconds per pair is plenty.
  • The same photo may appear several times, each time with different heatmaps. That's intended: judge every pair on its own, regardless of what you picked before.
  • There are 42 pairs, with a short break halfway.
