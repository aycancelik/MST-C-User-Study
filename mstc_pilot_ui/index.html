Study information

We are testing whether a mathematical score (MST-C) predicts which explanation heatmaps people find legible. You will compare pairs of heatmaps and answer a few short questions about your background and your impressions.

We record your choices, response times, a few interaction events (e.g. if the browser tab was hidden), your screen size and your questionnaire answers. We do not record your name or e-mail. Data are stored under a random participant code and used for research only. Participation is voluntary and you can stop at any time by closing the tab.

Step 2 of 3 · Your task

Click the heatmap you find more legible

For each photo, look at the two heatmaps and click the one that is more legible to you, meaning the one that's clearer and easier to read.

The question won't be repeated on every page. Just keep doing the same thing: one click, and the next pair appears straight away.

  • There are no right or wrong answers. We want your impression.
  • Don't overthink it, but do look at both. A few seconds per pair is plenty.
  • The same photo will appear several times, each time with different heatmaps. Judge every pair on its own.
  • There are 172 pairs. Now and then we'll briefly ask how focused you feel.
  • A few quality checks are included, so please stay attentive until the end.
